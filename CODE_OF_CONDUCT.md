# Code of conduct

bo-grid adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good
intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment of any kind is not
accepted.

It applies to every project space, including issues, pull requests,
discussions and review comments, and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the
maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/bonguynvan). Reports
are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break the code, and may restrict a participant's access.
